Practice

The Woodpecker Method

Tactics are trained by repetition, not by volume: you solve the same set of exercises over and over until the patterns come back on sight.

  • Build one set around your level and solve it from the easiest exercise to the hardest.
  • Go through the whole set at least four times, with no long breaks between passes.
  • Each pass gets less time than the one before — about half by the second, a third by the third, and as fast as you can hold your accuracy after that.
  • A missed exercise is marked and left behind: you see the solution, but there is no retry. Those misses are what measures the next pass.
  • Between passes the order is shuffled inside narrow rating bands, so what you recognise is the position and not the sequence.

The whole program is meant to fit in weeks, not months — the effect comes from keeping the passes close together.
