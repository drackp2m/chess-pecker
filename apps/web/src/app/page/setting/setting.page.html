Board

How the board plays. The strip below is a real exercise — take the black rook — so every setting here can be tried where it is chosen: miss on purpose and it takes your move back, miss it often enough and it plays the answer.

Move speed

The pace of everything the board plays by itself: the pause before a piece lights up, how long it stays lit, and how fast it then travels.

Move input

How you enter a move. Turning both off keeps clicking, so the board stays playable.

Sound

A piece landing picks a sound at random, never the one before it. Captures, check and mate have their own. Stepping back through a line plays them reversed.
